Open Android KB Bridge on the Flipper first, then tap Connect. Needs Bluefy on iPhone (Safari has no Web Bluetooth) or Chrome on Android.
Chat with Claude, or ask it to do something on the target. It replies here; only the keystrokes it proposes reach the keyboard, and only when you tap ▶ Send. Tap 📷 to show Claude the target's screen.
